Punching resistance of opening-adjacent slab–column connections

A transparent, closed-form research model: EN 1992-1-1:2004 evaluated on the full control perimeter, an opening factor with no fitted parameters, a strengthening recovery factor, and proposed partial factors.

This is not a design code.It is a research model calibrated on 80 specimens from 11 studies. The partial factors are research recommendations, not code-committee calibration. Flexural capacity and all applicable code checks must be verified separately.
